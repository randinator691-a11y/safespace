Our community rules

🔒 Protect privacy

Never ask someone for their address, school, phone number, password, private photos or other identifying information.

💚 Be kind

People may be sharing things that are difficult for them. Don't mock, insult, threaten or shame anyone.

🚩 Report problems

If something makes you uncomfortable or breaks the rules, report it rather than getting into an argument.

👥 No secret relationships

Don't pressure people to move conversations somewhere private. Keep support inside the moderated community.
